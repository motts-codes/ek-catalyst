# Progress log — 15–24 Nov 2026

What changed since the mid-November deploy pause, what is pending on the business side, and how to
pick things up. Everything below is committed on `main` (local only; nothing is pushed). Since
14 Nov the rule is **commit locally, deploy only when Arun asks**; the two stable aliases still

Run with `nvm use 24 && pnpm run dev`. Previous log: `docs/PROGRESS-2026-10-12.md`.

## Client QA round (23–24 Nov)

Seven items from EK's QA list, all addressed:

- **Cart Undo re-added the line three times.** The toast stayed open after Undo, so each click
  re-ran the restore. The toaster now closes on the first action click and ignores the rest
  (`vibes/soul/primitives/toaster`, `once()` helper).
- **Quantity +/− slow on repeated clicks.** Every click was its own queued server round trip.
  The counter steps instantly and sends one `set` update 500 ms after the last click. New `set`
  intent in `vibes/soul/sections/cart/schema.ts` and `app/.../cart/_actions/update-line-item.ts`.
- **Quantity can now be typed.** Number box (1–999); Enter or blur commits; invalid values revert.
- **Price My Kitchen colour step.** Always shown now, even for single-finish lines (Star shows the
  door as "Standard finish", pre-selected; Euro lists laminates as chips). The result card shows the
  chosen door's own kitchen photo instead of the generic shape render. Side cause fixed: in Pro mode
  the collection data dropped the kitchen photo, so `CabinetLine.photos` now carries both photos
  regardless of audience. Going back to step 1 and changing the line walks through colour again.
- **FAQ and glossary toggles.** Group headings use a ringed `+` / `−` (the shipping-policy FAQ
  pattern) instead of a `+` rotating to `×`.
- **Button hovers.** The complaint was missing motion, not colour. New
  `vibes/soul/lib/slide-fill.ts` exports the primitive's slide-in fill classes (`slideFill`,
  `slideFillPositioned`, `groupSlideFill`); applied to the 404 search button, showroom popup
  Directions, the showroom map-band card, the collection header switch and help pills, the cart
  Apply button and the blog card "Read more" pill. Nav items, icon buttons, chips and table rows are
  not buttons and keep their quiet tint.
- **Listing toolbar** shows "N products" (bold, foreground colour) on the left of category, brand and
  search grids (`Faceted.FacetedSearch.productsCount` message, `countLabel` prop).
- **Home search popover** stacks above the hero slider's play/pause controls (`z-50`).

## How to pick up

1. `nvm use 24 && pnpm run dev`; if pages 500 with `URLPattern is not a constructor`, Node is wrong.
2. Network: on the phone hotspot BigCommerce and Makeswift time out at random; retry before
   debugging.
3. Verify in Chrome with the Claude extension; tab groups vanish between sessions, so re-create
   with `navigate` or `tabs_context_mcp createIfEmpty`.
